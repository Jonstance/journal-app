Privacy Policy

Last updated  ·  31 May 2026

Your journal entries stay on your device. We never see them.
No account required. No analytics. No ads.
Backups are encrypted on-device before you choose where to save them.
AI prompts are optional and require your own API key.

What we collect

Nothing. Velvet Journal does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All journal entries, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device using on-device storage.

Encrypted backups

The backup feature lets you export your journal as an encrypted .journal file, secured with AES-256-GCM encryption and a password you choose. This file is created and encrypted entirely on your device. We never receive, transmit, or have access to it. You decide where to save it — iCloud, Google Drive, a USB drive, or anywhere else.

AI writing prompts (optional)

Velvet Journal includes an optional AI feature that generates context-aware writing prompts. To use it, you must provide your own Claude API key from Anthropic. This key is stored only on your device.

When you request a prompt, only the entry type (e.g. "Gratitude" or "Freeform") is sent to Anthropic's API — never the content of your entries. Velvet Journal does not operate any intermediary server; your device communicates directly with Anthropic. Anthropic's own Privacy Policy applies to those requests.

If you never set an API key, no data of any kind is sent to any external service.

Permissions

The app may request access to your microphone solely to record voice memos that are saved locally to your device. It requests internet access only when the optional AI feature is in use.

Children

Velvet Journal is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and note the change in the next app release.
